Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

ZWBDevDisk (the “App”) is a macOS disk analysis and cleanup utility developed by Zhu Wenbin. This policy explains what information the App processes, when it connects to third-party services, and how to contact the developer.

1. Information processed on your Mac

Disk scans, directory size calculations, installed app identification, network interface speed measurements, and process traffic measurements take place on your Mac. Scanning may read local folders selected by you or suggested by the App and process paths, file sizes, modification dates, app names, and scan results. The App stores settings, scan summaries, and cleanup history locally. It does not upload scanned files, file names, paths, app lists, or scan results to the developer.

Network speed and process traffic data are calculated using local macOS interfaces and system tools. They are displayed in the App and are not sent to the developer or used for advertising tracking.

2. Public IP lookups and third-party services

When you open the network details panel, the App may contact ipwho.is to look up your public IP address and country or region. If that request fails, the App may request your public IP address from ipify. The relevant service can see your public IP address and ordinary network request information; ipwho.is may return a country or region code. These requests do not include local scan results, file contents, or detailed process traffic data.

These services are independently operated. Their server logs, retention periods, and privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Review the latest information on the ipwho.is website and the ipify website. The App does not control how these services handle the requests they receive.

3. File access and cleanup

The App reads local directory metadata to calculate disk usage. macOS privacy permissions may restrict access to some folders; inaccessible items are not treated as empty folders. You select and confirm cleanup actions in the App. Recoverable items may be moved to the Trash. Items that the App explicitly identifies as permanently deleted cannot be recovered from the Trash after confirmation.

4. Advertising, analytics, and accounts

The App does not require an account, contain advertising or analytics SDKs, or use scan data for profiling or ad targeting.

5. Data retention and control

Local settings, scan summaries, and cleanup history are stored on your Mac and remain under your control. The App does not store this information on the developer’s servers. Removing an app from macOS may not automatically remove its local preferences or container data. To remove that data as well, manage the App’s local data through macOS before uninstalling it.

6. Children

The App is intended for macOS developers and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Policy updates

If the App’s data practices change, the developer will update this page and its effective date. Check this page for the current policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or privacy, contact the developer through GitHub Issues. Do not post passwords, keys, personal file paths, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

This page describes the privacy practices of ZWBDevDisk. Third-party services handle information according to their own published policies.
